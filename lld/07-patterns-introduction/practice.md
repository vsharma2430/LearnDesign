# Design Patterns Introduction — Practice

1. A program has one fixed tax rule. Should it use Strategy? Identify the evidence that would justify interchangeable policies.
2. Describe an anti-pattern you have encountered. What short-term benefit made it attractive, and what long-term cost followed?
3. For each requirement—construct objects based on input, add behavior around an object, notify many listeners—name a likely pattern family and a simpler alternative.
4. Pick a familiar pattern and explain its intent without naming its classes. Then identify its principal trade-off.
